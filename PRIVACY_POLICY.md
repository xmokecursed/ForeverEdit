<h1>ForeverEdit Privacy Policy</h1>

<p class="updated">Last updated: November 23, 2026</p>

<p>
  ForeverEdit is a browser extension that lets you visually customize
  the appearance of websites you visit. Your customizations stay on
  your device and are never sent to ForeverEdit or any third party.
</p>

<h2>What ForeverEdit stores</h2>

<p>
  When you use ForeverEdit to customize a website, your changes — including
  moved elements, hidden elements, pinned elements, edited text, and custom
  styles — are saved as a small set of instructions using your browser's
  built-in storage API (<code>chrome.storage.local</code>).
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Your data stays on your own device.</li>
  <li>Your data is never transmitted to ForeverEdit's developer, any third party, or any external server.</li>
  <li>Only ForeverEdit reads this data back to reapply your customizations when you revisit a website.</li>
</ul>

<p>
  If you turn on "sync" in the extension's settings, that data is mirrored
  using your browser vendor's own built-in sync feature (Chrome Sync or
  Firefox Sync via your Mozilla account) so your edits follow you across
  your own signed-in devices. This uses your browser's native sync
  infrastructure and is governed by your browser vendor's own privacy
  policy. ForeverEdit itself never sees or handles that data directly.
  This feature is off by default.
</p>

<h2>What ForeverEdit does not do</h2>

<ul>
  <li>It does not collect analytics, telemetry, or usage statistics.</li>
  <li>It does not track your browsing history.</li>
  <li>It does not read, log, or transmit the content of pages you visit, aside from the specific elements you choose to edit (which are stored locally as described above).</li>
  <li>It does not include ads, trackers, or any third-party scripts.</li>
  <li>It does not sell or share data with anyone, because it does not collect any data to begin with.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why the extension asks for broad permissions</h2>

<p>
  ForeverEdit requests access to all websites (<code>&lt;all_urls&gt;</code>)
  because its entire purpose is letting you edit the visual appearance of
  any site you personally choose to visit and customize. This permission
  is used exclusively to run the editor on the page you're actively
  looking at. It is never used to access pages in the background, monitor
  other tabs, or collect information you haven't explicitly asked the
  extension to store as part of an edit you made.
</p>

<h2>Exporting and deleting your data</h2>

<p>
  You can export everything ForeverEdit has stored as a plain JSON file
  at any time from the extension's Settings page. You can also delete
  all stored customizations for any site, or for all sites, from the
  same page. Uninstalling the extension removes all of its stored data
  from your browser.
</p>

<h2>Changes to this policy</h2>

<p>
  If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this
  page will change accordingly. Material changes will also be reflected
  in the extension's store listing.
</p>
